Questions people ask first

Still unsure? Message us on WhatsApp and a real person will answer.

What if the influencer never posts?

The money never leaves escrow. You get it back in full, or pick another influencer.

When does the influencer get paid?

72 hours after the sponsored post goes live (24 hours for stories), straight to their bKash or Nagad.

Do I approve the video before it goes up?

You approve the key points and the plan, then check the proof of the post. Influencers keep their own style, which is why their followers trust them. If you want a draft of a dedicated reel first, ask when you book: the influencer has to agree, and the fee may be higher.

How do you check influencers are real?

Influencers connect their Facebook, TikTok, YouTube or Instagram accounts directly, and our team reviews each profile before it goes live.

Do I need a website to run a campaign?

No. A Facebook or Instagram page is enough. Discount codes track orders that come through Messenger.

What is the smallest budget I can start with?

You can start with one influencer, from ৳9,200 (a ৳8,000 fee plus our 15%). Most first campaigns use 3 to 5 micro-influencers.
